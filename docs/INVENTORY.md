**Notes found while reading the code. They are carried into QUESTIONS and the ledger.**
- **A. Fake behaviour that must be replaced:**
  - `CruiseSearch` computes "N departures match" with a modulo formula and is not wired to data.
  - `CruiseSearch.Calendar` treats every matching weekday as a sailing day.
  - `NavBar` links are `href="#"` with local state only.
  - `BookingSummary`'s "Request to book" only flips local state.
- **B. Hard-coded figures and claims, all illustrative, which must come from data:**
  - `PriceAnatomy` factor amounts and copy, and its "per person, 4–5 nights" label.
  - `VESSELS`: guest, cabin and price ranges, and the meters.
  - `BookingSummary`: US$85 pp fees, US$60 pp gratuities, US$290 pp flights, a default of US$1,890, "Dahabiya Meroe", "12 Feb 2027", "free cancellation for 14 days" and "confirm within 24 hours".
  - `CabinPicker` default cabins.
  - `CruiseSearch` routes with weekday sailing days and guest ranges.
  - `ItineraryTimeline` default stops.
  - The previews use invented vessels ("Dahabiya Meroe", "Steamer Khedive", "Nile Aurora") with invented ratings and review counts, and "Browse 48 cruises".
- **C. Reduced-motion gaps:**
  - JS-driven motion ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`: `useCountUp` (rAF), `Gallery`'s 3D tilt, `Scene`'s pointer parallax (only the transition is shortened), `ItineraryTimeline` autoplay and smooth scrolling.
  - These must be fixed in the port (A11Y-004).
- **D. `CruiseCard` requires `price`.** Information-only listings need a "prices in preparation" state (DASH rule).
- **E. `VesselCompare` and `VesselTypeCard` support only sail/motor/steam.** The silo uses VesselCompare on `/vessels/felucca/` and `/vessels/compare/dahabiya-vs-felucca/`.
- **F. The focus ring relies on a `.nc-root` wrapper** (`:where(.nc-root) :focus-visible`). The port must apply it globally.
- **G. The `NavBar` defaults** ("Itineraries", "Journal") differ from the silo navigation (five pillars plus Cruises).
